### Added

- **Bounded file copies gain sparse preservation through additive APIs.** `SparseOptions` parses `sparse` alongside `times` and `links` in the existing repeatable, comma-delimited preservation argument. `export_file_with_sparse` seeks over zero chunks and sets the final logical length before sync and optional read-back verification; streaming writers still receive every byte. `import_file_with_sparse` omits zero writes only when the staged sink declares sparse support and can set its final length; missing support refuses before consuming input, and length failures abort before publication. Existing option literals and dense entry points stay compatible. Logical counts, progress and SHA-256 include holes as zeros. Command-level and recursive-tree policy wiring follows separately.
